# Muster v2 (Vergleich, Verträglichkeits-Test, eigene Alternativen) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A 90-day comparison per FODMAP group and ingredient on the Muster page, a guided one-item tolerance test, Alternativen ordered by the user's own verdicts, and Tipps that know all three — without any learned model.

**Architecture:** Core computes everything new in one pure module (`app/domain/pattern_stats.py`) served by one route (`pattern-stats`); the mirrored 30-day engine is untouched. Tests and alternative verdicts are per-user tables through PostgREST + RLS. The app renders the route's JSON, writes the two tables, ranks replacements with a pure function, and queues test cues in the Buddy hero's existing queue. The tips context reads the same module.

**Tech Stack:** Core: FastAPI, SQLAlchemy async, alembic, pytest, `fractions.Fraction`. App: Flutter, Riverpod, GoRouter, supabase_flutter, flutter_local_notifications (all present; no new package).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-insights-v2-design.md` (app repo, branch `docs/plan-insights-v2`). Section numbers below (§) refer to it.

**Phases (stop after any):** 1 statistics = IC1, IC2, IA1, IA2 · 2 guided tests = IC3, IC4, IA3, IA4, IA5 · 3 ranking = IC5, IA6, IA7 · 4 tips = IC6 · gates IC7, IA8 (run after the last phase built).

## Review Focus

1. **The user's diary has a day with meals but no gut feeling, and days with only medium entries.** Unrated days count nowhere; medium days count as „not hard" with their first entry as anchor. Tests in IC1 (`test_unrated_days_count_nowhere`, `test_medium_day_anchor_is_first_entry`).
2. **A test spans a DST switch or the user's timezone differs from the server's.** Phases follow local dates in `profiles.timezone`; exposure windows are 24 real hours, read on the wall clock. Tests in IC4 (`test_phases_follow_local_dates_across_dst`) and IA3 (`phase dates use the wall-clock today`).
3. **Two devices start a test at once, or the app starts one while another is active.** The second insert fails on the unique index; the app reloads and shows the running test, no error page. Tests in IC3 (`test_second_active_test_rejected`) and IA3 (`start conflict reloads and shows the running test`).
4. **A replacement is disliked and every other replacement of the group is disliked too.** Summaries show none, the detail shows them under „Nicht für mich", nothing disliked is ever first. Tests in IA6 (`all disliked: visible list empty`) and IA7.
5. **The route fails or the device is offline on the Muster page.** The 30-day sections render as before; only the new section shows „Der Vergleich ist gerade nicht verfügbar." with retry; no Buddy test cue is offered from stale data. Tests in IA2 (`route failure leaves the 30-day sections intact`) and IA5 (`no offer while stats are an error`).
